# Q-006: Rescore the six ILSIC-Multi results on the Lay-based label space, or keep them

Status: pending
Slug: slice-14
Raised by: wf-build, ticket 5 (full scoring report); wf-review (finding "Committed Multi scores not re-run on the Lay-based label space")
Blocking: no
Reversible: no

## Question

Spec decision 2 defines the label space as every statute string in the Lay train and test answers (543 labels). The six committed ILSIC-Multi scores used Lay train plus the Multi answers instead (540 labels). `ibid ilsic eval --space-from` now builds the spec's space, but using it on Multi means a second look at the Multi test for every layer. Do you want the Multi results rescored on the spec's space, or kept as they are?

## Why only you

Rescoring spends a second test look per layer under the one-look rule (spec decision 5), and that cannot be taken back once the scores are seen.

## Options

1. Keep the committed 540-label Multi scores, and state in the report and spec that they are not on the spec's space (the build's default). No new look is spent; the Multi primary reading is off-spec by 3 labels.
2. Rescore all six Multi results with `--space-from <Lay test> --rescore-test`. The ledger keeps the first scores visible beside the new ones, and the report shows the spec's space. Macro-F1 can only move slightly down, since 3 more zero-scoring labels join the average. Every layer is already far below the published Multi numbers, so no verdict changes.
3. Rescore and report both spaces side by side, the 540-label figures labelled as the first look. Same cost as option 2, with the most text in the report.

## Default taken

Option 1. The Multi block in `results/ilsic-report.md` is headed "540 labels", the gap is stated there, in spec decision 2 and in `.scratch/slice-14/pr.md`. No `--rescore-test` was used.

## If you answer differently

Options 2 or 3: run `ibid ilsic eval` six times on the Multi test with `--space-from` and `--rescore-test` (needs the data in the main checkout and the model files outside the repo, with `assoc-nb v2` rebuilt), which adds six ledger entries to `results/test-ledger-ilsic.json`, then regenerate `results/ilsic-report.md` and update the note in spec decision 2 and `pr.md`.
